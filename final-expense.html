Final Expense Insurance at a Glance

Final Expense Insurance is a simple, affordable way to make sure your loved ones aren't left with the financial burden of paying for funeral costs and other end-of-life expenses.

Coverage Details

✅ What It Covers

Final Expense Insurance helps your family handle important costs during a difficult time.

⚱️

Funeral & Burial Costs

Covers traditional burial or cremation expenses, ensuring your final wishes are honored without financial stress on your family.

📄

Outstanding Bills

Helps pay final medical bills, credit cards, or small debts so your loved ones aren't burdened with unexpected financial obligations.

🎁

Legacy Gift

Leaves extra funds behind for your loved ones as a meaningful gift to help them during their time of loss.

Plan Benefits

✅ Key Features

Our Final Expense plans are designed with your needs in mind.

💵

Affordable Coverage

Designed for seniors and those on a fixed income. Get the protection you need at a price you can afford.

📋

Simplified Issue

Usually no medical exam required – just quick health questions to get you covered fast.

🛡️

Permanent Protection

Coverage lasts for life, as long as premiums are paid. Your family will always be protected.

📊

Level Premiums

Rates never increase, and coverage never decreases. Lock in your rate today for lifetime stability.

Living Benefits

✅ Living Benefits Options

Access your benefits when you need them most – varies by plan.

🏥

Terminal Illness Rider

Access benefits if life expectancy is 12 months or less. Get the support you need during difficult times.

🩺

Chronic Illness Rider

Provides funds if unable to perform 2 of 6 daily living activities. Maintain your independence with financial support.

❤️

Critical Illness Rider

Early access to benefits if diagnosed with serious conditions like cancer, heart attack, stroke, ALS, or organ failure.

💡

Why Families Choose It

Final Expense Insurance gives your loved ones peace of mind, dignity, and financial relief. It allows them to focus on honoring your memory instead of worrying about how to pay for funeral arrangements and expenses.
